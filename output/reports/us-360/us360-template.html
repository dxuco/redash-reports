US 360 — traffic, acquisition and revenue

January–August 2025 vs 2026 · GA4 property 339919146 + Redash query 1732 · whale excluded

How "US" is defined here. The revenue figures cover the US bloc: players whose player_country is United States of America or VPN Player. The VPN bucket is ~90% US and is the largest single country bucket in the cache — bigger than the US bucket itself — so a US read that drops it understates the business by more than half. GA4 cannot make the same join: it geolocates by IP, so a US player on a VPN is counted under the VPN's exit country. Traffic and revenue in this report therefore cover overlapping but not identical populations, and the traffic side is the narrower of the two.

01The headline

Every funnel measure fell. Every money measure rose. This is the whole report in one line: the traffic that left was traffic that did not spend.

02Gross and net gaming revenue, by month

Negative months stack below the zero rule rather than being clamped. January 2026 was a real loss month — a single VPN-bucket cohort ran −$360k GGR — and averaging it away would hide the most volatile thing in the data.

03The funnel

Depositors held; first-time depositors halved. The gap between those two lines is the entire risk in this report.

04GA4 sessions by channel — US only

Referral collapsed from 56,107 sessions in January 2025 to a few thousand a month. Two spikes are tracking artifacts, not demand — see the note below.

Two spikes to discount. November 2025 carries 143,645 Unassigned US sessions and August 2026 carries 133,481 Direct — each several times the surrounding months, and neither has any answering movement in deposits or GGR. Treat both as instrumentation events. The apparent "recovery" in July–August 2026 is almost entirely this.

06Direct, affiliate and streamer

Streamer traffic loses money. It grew depositors 48% and deposits 390% — and returned negative GGR and NGR both years, −$1,704 and −$2,967 in 2026. More of it makes the number worse, not better.
