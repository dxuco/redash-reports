Definitions. Metrics are life-to-date since FTD (the FTD is a player's first-ever deposit, so summing the full data window equals lifetime-since-FTD). Deposit / Deposit count / Adj GGR / Bonus cost all from Redash query 1732. Adj GGR is Redash's own adjusted figure, not derived by subtracting bonus cost. Rail (Fiat/Crypto) is the payment method on the player's first deposit row carrying a payment method. Aff name is the affiliate captured on the FTD-day row itself; blank shows as "Direct". Active bonus = count of Smartico bonus grants that reached REDEEMED status (bonus_status_id 3); Smartico's own cost field is unreliable in this warehouse (always books 0), so bonus cost shown is Redash's realized figure, while the grant/redemption timeline in the player detail comes from Smartico.
